Donner: six installed translation review editions

Installed 8 September 2026. These are OCR review editions, not fully proofread transcriptions. Historical spelling is preserved. Repairs address displaced verse endings, wrapped words, verse numbering and speaker/stanza labels. The Harvard OCR remains the copy text; New York OCR differences are available for review. Each translation uses its own Donner line citations, with corresp to containing Smyth card passages. These are not exact line-for-line correspondences.
