## Verification and corrections

- Inspected all page tops and appendix openings against the images. Opening pages without a printed numeral were identified by the appendix heading and adjacent numbered pages.
- Replaced the blurred Libation Bearers p. 154 duplicate at PDF 170 with its clear rescan at PDF 172.
- Corrected Eumenides mappings to PDF 228–232 (printed 210–214). A constant volume-I offset would have selected two pages of reading notes and omitted the final two metrical pages.
- Images are faithful PDF renders. No graphic reconstruction or alteration of the quantities was applied.
- JSON inventory records work, volume, printed page, actual PDF page, scan caveats and an SHA-256 checksum for every image.
- Seven TEI facsimile inventories supply a surface and graphic for every page; these are not registered as seven completed translation editions.

## Remaining editorial work

The gallery is complete; the broader Donner edition is not. Agamemnon is the installed OCR review edition, with its existing 99 Smyth cards and four opening metrical attachments. The other six translations are now installed as OCR review editions, with 487 Smyth navigation cards, independent Donner line citations, and 63 selected located reading notes. See Six-plays-review.html for their inventories and unresolved readings.

Detailed machine-readable transcription of metrical schemes and verse-level attachment beyond the opening Agamemnon examples remain pending. The printed verse labels are Donner’s references and must be checked separately against Hermann and Smyth.

Unattributed reading notes remain in `readings-both-witnesses.json` and the pagewise two-witness review; Agamemnon has its more specific `Agamemnon-unattributed-readings.json`. Explanatory and metrical OCR from both witnesses remain archived without speculative corrections.
