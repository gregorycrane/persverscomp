Donner’s Euripides — editorial inventory

Eighteen OCR review editions installed in the Perseus Multitext Viewer. Detailed proofreading remains unfinished. Harvard OCR is the running-text source. Independent BSB scans and OCR supply a second witness for all three volumes. Both sources contain OCR errors; agreement alone is not treated as proof of correctness.
