Reading the grid

Rows are grammatical slots. In the one-stemtype layout, columns are tense–mood–voice combinations. In the comparison layout, choose one exact tense, mood, and voice; columns then show every attested stemtype for that paradigm. Each cell ranks attested surface forms, initially showing only the most frequent. Click a lemma to switch to its own forms while preserving the corpus and morphology filters. Counts combine OGA morphology with Morpheus analyses that agree on lemma and part of speech. Short definitions are from Logeion Short Definitions (Greek) by Helma Dik.
